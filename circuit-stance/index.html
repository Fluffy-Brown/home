YOUR STRATEGY STARTS BEFORE THE MATCH

Build the behaviour behind the battle.

You take the role of a super AI in a post-apocalyptic world, programming a robot squad to compete for resources. The modular chip system is the centre of the build: combine chip actions to create the behaviours you want to test.

01

Choose a combatant and a setup

Choose a combatant, inspect its stats, and use the hexagonal chip board to prepare your setup. Think about who you are building around and which chip actions should form the plan before you head into the arena.

02

Give the combination a purpose

Modular actions allow different tactical builds. Treat the arrangement as one plan: decide what you want your robot to do, try a combination that expresses that idea, and use the match to judge whether it works.

WATCH YOUR DECISIONS MEET AN OPPONENT

Take the build into the arena.

Quick, complex turn-based matches put your choices to the test. Preparation and testing connect directly: the chips you choose become a strategy to evaluate against the next arena challenge.

01

Read the result

Arena screenshots place the combatants alongside health bars and visible chip boards. Follow those changing battle states and compare the outcome with the plan you built. Did the combination produce the behaviour you were aiming for?

02

Carry a question into the next build

A match is feedback for the next experiment. Reconsider a chip choice or the overall combination, then test again. The game's iterative strategy testing is about experimenting, optimizing and validating your tactics through play.

EXPERIMENTATION WITH SOMETHING AT STAKE

Refine the plan. Pursue the next rank.

Circuit Stance is a strategy roguelike with a tiered scoring system. Victories move your rank upward; losses bring challenges and the risk of starting over.

01

One useful testing cycle

Start with a chip arrangement, take it into a match, observe the result, and revise the build before another test. Compare what happened after the change, rather than assuming a new combination must be better.

02

Progress through tactical learning

Ranking gives each encounter weight, while repeated testing helps you understand your build. The next decision can draw on the last battle: which ideas held up, which need another attempt, and what to change for the next opponent.

The visual world uses vibrant flat 2D anime art, with distinct combatant designs and readable chip boards. Explore the original character stories below for more of the personalities behind that world.

Vampire

Vayne

  • Blood

Read Vayne's story

Vayne was once the ultimate ruler of the magical realm, a symbol of immortality and terror. Cold and ruthless, he struck fear into all who opposed him. But when the multiverse collapsed, even his so-called eternal body was nearly destroyed. Facing death for the first time, he was offered salvation by the laboratory—a mechanical body to survive this chaotic era.

Bound by this 'rescue', Vayne regained power but at the cost of suppressing his violent nature, becoming a controlled warrior for the lab. Though he appears loyal, his inner rage simmers, waiting for the moment to break free and reclaim his dark dominion.

Gunslinger

Selene

  • Gun
  • Dexterity
Read Selene's story

Selene was once a dedicated human patrol officer, walking city streets in her black uniform, her gaze steady yet warm. She safeguarded not just order but the people who relied on her. When catastrophe struck, tearing Adar apart, she became a beacon of hope, leading survivors through ruins to a laboratory-built refuge. Exhausted, she ensured every life was safe before giving her own, her name becoming a symbol of courage.

But this wasn’t the end. To inspire humanity, the laboratory resurrected her as a machine. Though she struggled with her cold, metallic body, Selene embraced her new purpose: to protect and fight for survival, unwavering in her mission.

Elyse fights with unmatched precision, wielding magic and steel as if solely for the laboratory’s honor. Yet, her true drive lies in uncovering the world’s hidden truths. Before the disaster, Galenna was a simple guardian robot, protecting her city with unwavering resolve. When chaos shattered the world, she fought valiantly but was powerless against the destruction.
