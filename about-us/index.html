Solo mining & toy-market adventure

MunchMiner ↗

Put on the red helmet and work for Marksa, a would-be world conqueror with a village to look after. Shape tunnels, connect powered stations, and let their Dudu carts bring materials home. Back in town, turn your haul into toys, choose your stall's stock and work the evening crowd before planning the next expedition.

  • Excavate a persistent side-view mine
  • Connect tracks, power and automatic freight
  • Learn recipes and run your toy stall

Tactical programming & strategy

Circuit Stance ↗

In this turn-based strategy roguelike, your plan begins with the robot's AI. Combine modular chip actions to build behaviours, take the result into the arena and watch how it performs. Each match gives you another chance to reconsider your choices; victories move you up the game's tiered ranking, while losses put the run under pressure.

  • Build behaviours with modular AI chips
  • Test tactical choices in the arena
  • Rework your build and pursue a higher rank
